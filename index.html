04 / ABOUT THE ANALYSIS

Methodology & notes

What was calculated and how to interpret the numbers.

How are scores calculated?

Total marks = Math + Science + English + Computer. A student's percentage equals (Total marks ÷ 400) × 100. Subject averages are the mean marks across all 10 students.

How are the grade categories assigned?

Excellent: 80% or above. Good: 70–below 80%. Average: 50–below 70%. Needs Improvement: below 50%, consistent with the Excel project grading logic.

Is this dashboard interactive?

Navigation links and expandable notes work with HTML alone. The charts, KPIs, and records are static and won't change when the Excel workbook is edited. JavaScript is not used anywhere on this page.

Where does the data come from?

These are 10 anonymized sample student records from an institute Excel assignment. Attendance is a supplied 0–100 metric, not independently verified calendar-based attendance. The original calculations and formulas can be explored in the downloadable Excel workbook.
